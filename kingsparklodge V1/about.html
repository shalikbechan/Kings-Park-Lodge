About Us

Straightforward, Comfortable Accommodation

Kings Park Lodge is a small, independently-run accommodation property in Morningside, Durban, built around one idea: a clean, comfortable, well-located place to stay shouldn't cost a fortune.

Who we are

Kings Park Lodge is located in Morningside, Durban — a well-connected, central suburb close to the city's major venues and attractions. We provide affordable accommodation for guests visiting Durban for business, events, leisure or a short getaway.

Our rooms are comfortable and practical, each with air conditioning, a television and a private bathroom. Guests also have access to free Wi-Fi, free on-site parking, and a shared kitchen and eating area for preparing meals or relaxing between plans.

We keep things simple: clear pricing, a convenient location, and the essentials done properly.
